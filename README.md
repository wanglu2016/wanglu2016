# Wang Lu

王路，作家，阿毗达磨学者。著有《红楼碧看》《韩愈传》《俱舍九题》等。得到APP《深度思考日课》《金刚经50讲》《情绪觉知》主理人。

Writer and independent scholar working on Chinese intellectual history and Abhidharma.

I have contributed to the Digital Dictionary of Buddhism (DDB), and I am interested in experimenting with human–AI collaboration on research and problem solving across disciplinary boundaries.
